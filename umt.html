About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

University Overview & History

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as the Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) in Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu, providing facilities for practical training for students of the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes.

In May 1999, the Cabinet of Malaysia approved the establishment of Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) as an autonomous campus before it was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001. On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was officially granted full university status and rebranded as Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.

Today, UMT is recognized as Malaysia's premier university focused on marine science, aquatic resources, and environmental sustainability, situated along the scenic coast of the South China Sea.
